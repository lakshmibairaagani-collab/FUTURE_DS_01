# FUTURE_DS_01

## Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Internship

This repository contains the completed tasks for the Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship.

## Task 1 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

A Power BI dashboard analyzing business sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, categories, regions, and key business KPIs.

## Task 2 – Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

A Power BI dashboard analyzing customer churn and retention, including tenure, contracts, payment methods, services, and high-risk customer segments.

## Task 3 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Analysis

A Power BI dashboard analyzing the marketing funnel from impressions to customers, including conversion rates, channel performance, marketing spend, cost per lead, and cost per customer.
